\begin{definition}
Let $f : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a function that is infinitely many times differentiable on some open set $\Omega$. Assume $c \in \Omega$.

Then the function series
\[
\sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{f^{(k)}(c)}{k!} (x - c)^k
\]
is called the \emph{Taylor series} of $f$ around $c$.
\end{definition}
