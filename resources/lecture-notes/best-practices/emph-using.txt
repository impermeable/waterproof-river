a there-exists statement}\label{it:bp-using-exists}
If you know that a there-exists statement such as
\begin{mcodenumbered}
	\label{eq:exists-statement-to-use}
	\begin{split}
		& \existstext a \in A, \\
		&\qquad (\ldots)
	\end{split}
\end{mcodenumbered}
holds, you can {\bf use} it as follows
\begin{mcode}
	\text{Obtain an } a \in A \text{ according to } (\ref{eq:exists-statement-to-use})
\end{mcode}
or as 
\begin{mcode}
	\text{Obtain an } a \in A \text{ such that } (\ldots) \text{ according to } (\ref{eq:exists-statement-to-use})
\end{mcode}
or just
\begin{mcode}
	\text{Obtain such an } a \in A.
\end{mcode}
