To directly prove a statement 
\begin{mcode}
	\begin{split}
		&\existstext a \in A,\\
		&\quad\quad ( \dots )
	\end{split}
\end{mcode}
you make a choice for $a$ and write
\begin{mcode}
	\text{Choose } a := \dots
\end{mcode}
Next, check that with you choice, indeed $a \in A$.
If this is obvious, you can write
\begin{mcode}
	\text{Indeed, } a \in A.
\end{mcode}
Otherwise, if it is not immediately obvious that $a \in A$, write down a proof of this fact.

After mentioning or proving that $a \in A$, you continue to prove $( \dots )$.
If the proof of $a \in A$ was long, it is good to state first what you have to show, namely $( \dots )$.
