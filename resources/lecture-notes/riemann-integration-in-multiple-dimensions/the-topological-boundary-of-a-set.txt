The topological boundary of a set in a metric space is defined as those points that are neither in the interior of the set, nor in the interior of the complement of the set. For the subsets of $(\mathbb{R}^d, \| \cdot \|_2)$, this comes down to the following definition.

\begin{definition}[Topological boundary]
	Let $E$ be a subset of the normed vector space $(\mathbb{R}^d, \| \cdot \|)$. The boundary of $E$ is defined as
	\[
	\partial E := \mathbb{R}^d \setminus 
	\big( (\mathrm{int} \ E  ) 
	\cup
	(\mathrm{int} (\mathbb{R}^d \setminus E)) \big).
	\]
\end{definition}
