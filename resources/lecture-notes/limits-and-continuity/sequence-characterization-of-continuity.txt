As with many concepts in the course, continuity is conveniently probed with sequences.

\begin{theorem}[Sequence characterization of continuity]
	\label{th:sequence-char-cont}
Let $(X, \dist_X)$ and $(Y, \dist_Y)$ be metric spaces.
Let $D \subset X$ and let $f : D \to Y$ be a function.
Let $a \in D$.
The function $f$ is continuous in $a$ if and only if
\[
\begin{split}
& \foralltext \text{ sequences } (x^{n}) \text{ in } D \text{ converging to } a,\\
& \quad \lim_{n \to \infty} f(x^{n}) = f(a).
\end{split}
\]	
\end{theorem}
