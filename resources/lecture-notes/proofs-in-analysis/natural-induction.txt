To show a statement
\begin{mcode}
	\begin{split}
		&\foralltext n \in \mathbb{N}, \\
		&\quad\quad P(n)
	\end{split}
\end{mcode}
you can use natural induction. Here, $P(n)$ is a statement depending on $n$. The template is as follows:

\begin{mcode}
	\begin{split}
		& \text{We use induction on } n \in \mathbb{N}.\\
		&\quad\quad \text{We first show the base case, i.e.~that } P(0) \text{ holds.}\\
		&\quad\quad \quad\quad \textit{... insert here a proof of $P(0)$ ...}\\
		&\quad\quad \text{We now show the induction step.}\\
		&\quad\quad \quad \text{Let } k \in \mathbb{N} \text{ and assume that } P(k) \text{ holds.}\\
		&\quad\quad\quad \text{We need to show that } P(k + 1) \text{ holds.}\\
		&\quad\quad\quad\quad \textit{... insert here a proof of $P(k+1)$ ...}
	\end{split}
\end{mcode}
