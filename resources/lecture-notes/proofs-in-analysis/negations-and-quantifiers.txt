We write the negation of a mathematical statement $( \textellipsis )$ as $\neg ( \dots )$. The so-called \emph{De Morgan's laws} specify how quantifiers behave under negation.

The statement
\begin{mcode}
\neg \left( \foralltext a \in A, \ (\textellipsis) \right)
\end{mcode}
is equivalent to
\begin{mcode}
\existstext a \in A, \left( \neg ( \textellipsis )\right).
\end{mcode}
Similarly, the statement
\begin{mcode}
\neg \left( \existstext a \in A, \ ( \dots )\right)
\end{mcode}
is equivalent to
\begin{mcode}
\foralltext a \in A, \ ( \neg ( \dots ) ).
\end{mcode}
