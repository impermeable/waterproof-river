The following theorem is a direct consequence of Theorem \ref{th:preservation-differentiability} on the preservation of differentiability. 

\begin{theorem}
Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of functions from $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$.
Suppose
\begin{enumerate}
	\item for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the function $f_n$ is continuously differentiable on $\Omega$.
	\item the function series
	\[
	\sum_{k=0}^\infty f_k
	\]
	converges pointwise to a function $g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$.
	\item the function series
	\[
	\sum_{k=0}^\infty f_k'
	\]
	converges uniformly.
\end{enumerate}	
Then the function $g$ is differentiable on $\Omega$ as well and for all $x \in \Omega$,
\[
g'(x) = \sum_{k=0}^\infty f_k'(x)  
\]
\end{theorem}
